Scriptorium 0.5.0

• Updates itself: Scriptorium checks GitHub when it starts and asks before installing a new version. Every download is checked (SHA-256) and your songs are backed up first.
• Songs on two computers: songs added, changed or deleted at church and at home are merged song by song instead of one copy replacing the other. If the same song was changed on both, the newer version is kept and the other goes into the song book "Conflicte la îmbinare / Merge conflicts". Set it up in Settings › Song sync & backups (a shared folder such as OneDrive/Google Drive/a USB stick, or a file).
• Backups of the songs before every update and merge, with Restore.
• Clock: 24-hour or 12-hour (AM/PM) – Settings › General or Clock, key & page.
• Runs on Windows, macOS (Apple Silicon and Intel) and Fedora/Linux (.rpm or a one-line install).
• Security fixes: the program window can no longer be taken over by a dropped or linked file, downloaded tools are checksum-verified, links pasted into the downloader can't run commands, and song/list files from other computers can't inject anything into the screen. Electron updated to version 45 (security updates).
• Fedora: the projector window now opens on the projector screen (it could land on the wrong screen under Wayland).

Your songs from 0.4 are kept and upgraded automatically the first time 0.5 starts.
